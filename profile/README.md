# Green Reliable Software Budapest

**Building software that is greener, more reliable and more open.**

Green Reliable Software Budapest is a Budapest-based technical community exploring how software, infrastructure and open technology can better respond to environmental and real-world constraints.

We bring together developers, researchers, students and practitioners through technical sessions, open-source work and community programmes.

## Current

### Hack for Earth 2026 — Greener Fields

A month-long **Green + Open Software** initiative exploring how technology can make the systems we depend on more:

**SECURE · EFFICIENT · RESILIENT**

**[Join HACK4EARTH 2.0 on Kaggle →](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/hack-4-earth-2-0-greener-fields)**

Technical materials and examples live in the [2026 repository](https://github.com/Green-Reliable-Software-Budapest/Hack-for-Earth-Green-Fields-2026).

## What we work on

**Green software**  
Measuring and reducing the environmental demands of software and computing systems.

**Reliable systems**  
Designing software and infrastructure that remain trustworthy under real-world constraints.

**Open source & digital commons**  
Supporting open technical knowledge, maintainership and collaborative development.

**Resilient infrastructure**  
Exploring software for energy, food, water, communications, climate and other essential systems.
